## 4. Decisive test: can the missing uniform inverse constants rescue (6)?

No, within the retained continuum framework. Here are lower bounds on the
actual operator norms, not assumed values for their unknown upper bounds.

### 4.1 Positive transfers cannot be arbitrarily small

Take a smooth radial bump chi on a compact shell away from bolt and infinity,
and u_N=chi cos(N rho_bar). Set q_N=K u_N using the ACTUAL nonlocal K.
The original positive realization gives K^-1 q_N=u_N. Its nonlocal tails
are retained. At each s=0,2,4, the qualitative bound Q:H_W^(s+2)->H_W^(s+2)
from the retained graph argument makes Q u_N=O(N^(s+2)). Local lower terms
and differentiated coefficients are O(N^(s+3)). The full covariant leading
tensor is the unit radial covector tensor power. Averaging cos^2 gives

    ||D0 u_N||H_W^s = N^(s+4)(||W chi||/sqrt(2)+o(1)),
    ||K u_N||H_W^(s+2)
        =N^(s+4)(||W(1+alpha)chi||/sqrt(2)+o(1)).

Since |alpha|<1e-32, any valid R_s satisfies

    R_s >=1/(1+1e-32)>.99, hence R>.97.                    (10)

Only qualitative boundedness of Q is used in this limit; Q is not dropped
from a physical solve or commuted through W. Already R Cstart cannot fit
Bc for epsilon_c<=1: it would require epsilon_c>1e7, using even the looser
g<1.6e-33. Thus computing R_s upper bounds cannot rescue the present low
mixed allowance, even if the entire high TT term is artificially set to zero.

### 4.2 The higher TT inverse cannot supply a compensating power of g

The [actual finite TT normal](finite-comparison-tt-realization-and-range.md)
is 9 N_T on the physical critical line s=3/2+i nu. With r_nu=nu^2+9/4,

    d(nu)=9[A_T r_nu+d0 r_nu(r_nu-2) H_M(s)],
    A_T=1/(48pi g)+23/(8640pi^2), d0=1/(480pi^2),
    H_M(s)=Re psi(1/2+i nu)-(pi/2)sech(pi nu)+kappa_M,
    39/5<kappa_M<54/5.                                    (11)

It is positive on this line. Retain the scalar-c factor nine, rather than
replacing the scalar pairing by a tensor Gram matrix. The digamma series
and half-integer value are [DLMF 5.7.6](https://dlmf.nist.gov/5.7.E6) and
[5.4.13](https://dlmf.nist.gov/5.4.E13), inspected 2026-10-22. Subtraction gives

    Re psi(1/2+i nu)=psi(1/2)
       +sum_(n>=0) nu^2/[(n+1/2)((n+1/2)^2+nu^2)].

The summand is positive decreasing. Its first term is <2 and its integral
from 1/2 to infinity is (1/2)log(1+4nu^2). Since psi(1/2)<0,
Re psi<2+log(1+2nu). Choose ONE frequency nu=g^(-1/2), not a sampled
supremum. Then nu>100, nu<1/(3e-17), log(1+2nu)<40, and H_M<54.
The log bound is certified by a positive rational partial sum for exp(40).
Also g A_T<.007 and r_nu/nu^2<1.001. Consequently

    d(nu)/nu^4
      <9[.007(1.001)+54(1.001)^2/(480*9)] <1/5.           (12)

Do not substitute a Fourier norm for the physical Sobolev sum. In the
asymptotic unit-curvature orthonormal frame e0,e1,e2,e3, radial frame
transport is zero, while each tangential connection rotates e0 and ea.
On rank j covariant tensors its generator norm is at most j. If A_j is
the full j-derivative tensor amplitude of z^s, then

    A0=1, A_(j+1)<=sqrt(|s|^2+3j^2) A_j,
    A8>=|s|^8>=nu^8,
    sum_(j=0)^4 A_j<=sum_(j=0)^4(nu+8)^j<2nu^4.          (13)

The last inequality holds for every nu>=100 by monotonicity in 1/nu
after division by nu^4. Thus the physical H8/H4 mode ratio is greater
than nu^4/2, and division by (12) is greater than 5/2.

To transfer this LOWER bound to the actual operator, put x=-log z and use
real packets c_(n,L)=L^-1/2 z^(3/2) cos(nu x) chi((x-n)/L). They vanish
near the bolt and have zero mass column. Define f_(n,L)=Dbar c_(n,L),
including its actual nonlocal tails; Dbar^-1 f_(n,L)=c_(n,L).
First send n to infinity at fixed L. The retained two-power kernel
improvement, differentiated through output order four, tends to zero
on this packet; the separated critical tails have exponential margin
min(4-3/2,1+3/2)=5/2 and control the compact output. Physical measures
and ordered tensor coefficients converge to their normal limits.
Then send L to infinity: the smooth Fourier envelope concentrates at
the two frequencies +/-nu, d is smooth there, and its polynomial-log
growth is dominated by the rapidly decreasing envelope. Cosine averaging
gives A_j/sqrt(2) in every derivative norm and the same factor for the
output. This avoids an unjustified equality of real and complex operator
norms for a sum of Hilbert norms. Therefore

    C_D4>2.                                               (14)

This uses the existing finite-order actual kernel mapping and realization,
not an assertion that the global operator equals its normal operator.
It gives no numerical upper bound or rate of packet convergence. If that
